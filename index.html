questions

Good to know.

Does online tutoring really work?

Yes. J has taught online since 2012, and parents who were skeptical at first say the results changed their minds. Lessons run on Microsoft Teams, so all you need is a computer, tablet or phone.

How fast will we see results?

Most students improve by at least two letter grades after three sessions. Every student is different, and the best results come from regular weekly lessons.

What's the difference between live lessons and videos?

Live lessons are private and built around your student's exact homework and test. Videos follow the full course in order and are great for staying ahead or reviewing before finals.

How do the video sessions work?

Each session is about two hours with 5-minute breaks every 30 minutes. For best results, watch it straight through like a live class, then print the session notes at the end.

Do you offer help in Spanish?

Yes. The Algebra 1 group programs are also offered in Spanish. Sí, también ofrecemos tutorías en español.

What ages do you work with?

From 4th grade through college, including AP courses, SAT math and college calculus and physics.
